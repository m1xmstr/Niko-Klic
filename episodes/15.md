# Episode 15: Stop the Loop!

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** stop loop  
**Learning goal:** Recognize that a repeating loop can be stopped.

Klic repeats hops while RUN is true, then the STOP event ends repetition.

## Learning path

After Episode 2 loops and Episode 10 events.

## Try it together

Repeat clapping until a partner says stop.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What ends the repetition?

**Answer:** The stop instruction.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

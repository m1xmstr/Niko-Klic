# Episode 4: Klic Gets Stuck!

[Watch Episode 4](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YDvP1hynkV4)

**Concept:** Sequences  
**Learning goal:** Put dependent steps in the order that makes the goal possible.

Klic cannot cross a missing bridge. Nico moves BUILD before CROSS; the bridge forms, then Klic crosses.

## Learning path

After Episode 1; introduces ordered steps used in Episode 8.

## Try it together

Draw a river and use paper rectangles to make a bridge before moving a toy across.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Which step must happen first?

**Answer:** Build the bridge, then cross.

## For grown-ups

These short stories introduce a concept through visible action. Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode, not the syntax of every programming language. Pause, predict, and retell the sequence together. No account, personal information, passwords, or paid tool is needed for this activity.

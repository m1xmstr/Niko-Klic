# Episode 14: Find It Fast!

[Watch Episode 14](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/3PRYbOXCw-c)

**Concept:** searching  
**Learning goal:** Search a collection until the requested item is found.

A pointer checks red then blue and stops when the target matches.

## Learning path

After Episode 11 lists.

## Try it together

Find a named color among three paper cards.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Why does the search stop at blue?

**Answer:** Blue matches the requested color.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

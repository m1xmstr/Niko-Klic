# Episode 30: Can Klic Reach Ten? Count with Integers

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** integers  
**Learning goal:** Integers include zero and counting numbers, and their negatives.

Klic crosses ten numbered stepping stones. Integers include zero and counting numbers, and their negatives.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 2, 6. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Try it together

Arrange ten paper pads numbered 1–10. Start before the first pad at zero and count each step.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What integer comes after nine?

**Answer:** Ten. Nine plus one is ten.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

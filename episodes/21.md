# Episode 21: Understanding What We’ve Learned

[Watch Episode 21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwPC3yVyELY)

**Concept:** review  
**Learning goal:** Recall twenty coding and digital-safety lessons and explain an example.

Nico and Klic revisit all twenty lessons on a large classroom screen, with one clear term, code and animated example at a time.

## Learning path

Review of Episodes 1–20. Pause at any term and revisit its original lesson.

## Try it together

Choose a favorite term, explain it in your own words, and act out its example with a grown-up. Use pretend information only.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What is the difference between a loop and a function?

**Answer:** A loop repeats steps. A function groups named steps that can be called; those steps can also contain a loop.

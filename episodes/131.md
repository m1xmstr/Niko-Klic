# Episode 131 — Does AI Need That Detail? AI Privacy

Status: [Watch the published lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_f6ROOucKdc). Public playback and manual English captions verified October 28, 2026.

## What we are learning

Before sharing a prompt with an AI tool, remove unnecessary private details and ask a grown-up to review it. A placeholder is a stand-in label; it does not guarantee anonymity or make every request safe.

Prerequisites: [121 — Meet AI](121.md) and [126 — Check the Answer](126.md).

## The postcard puzzle

Nico types a fictional postcard prompt while Klic reviews which details the task needs. The large screen shows an **unsent, fictional prompt preview**, not an AI response. No information is submitted to a model.

The invented address below is a teaching example, not a real contact detail:

```text
Write a postcard from
12 Pretend Lane.
```

The writing task does not need a street address. Replace it with a placeholder:

```text
Write a postcard from
[PLACE].
```

Changed case: replace a recipient name too:

```text
Write a postcard to
[FRIEND] from [PLACE].
```

These are preview text examples. There is no generated output and no claim that a tool automatically removes private information.

## Predict and explain

- What could replace the address? **A placeholder such as [PLACE], or a general fictional setting.**
- What could replace a person's name? **[FRIEND].**
- Does one placeholder hide every private detail? **No. Review the whole request; other details may still identify someone.**
- What should you do before sharing? **Pause and ask a grown-up to help review the request and decide whether to use the tool.**

## Grown-up activity

On paper, make a pretend postcard request using only invented details. Cover the pretend address with a [PLACE] slip and the pretend recipient with [FRIEND]. Read the entire request together and decide which details the writing task actually needs. Keep the exercise on paper; you do not need to open an AI account or submit anything.

Never ask a child to write or share their real address, school, password, contact information, or another person's private details for this activity.

**Recap:** Pause. Replace private details. Review before sending.

# Episode 11: Klic Makes a List

[Watch Episode 11](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/fCqa0Avh3l4)

**Concept:** lists  
**Learning goal:** Recognize a list as an ordered collection of items.

A ball, car and kite enter three ordered list slots.

## Learning path

After strings and variables.

## Try it together

Arrange three toys in a row and name them in order.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** How many items are in the list?

**Answer:** Three: ball, car and kite.

# Episode 36: Klic Cannot Stop! An Infinite Loop

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** infinite loops  
**Learning goal:** A forever loop repeats until something stops it.

Klic keeps bouncing because a loop has no end condition. A forever loop repeats until something stops it.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 2, 15. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Try it together

Pretend to hop in a loop on a safe flat space. Agree on a stop signal before starting.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Does this forever loop stop on its own?

**Answer:** No. An outside stop or a programmed stopping condition is needed.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

# Episode 36: Klic Cannot Stop Hopping! The Forever Loop

[Watch Episode 36](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ExzmqzCsWj0)

**Concept:** forever loop  
**Learning goal:** A forever loop needs a way to stop when we need one.

A forever loop keeps Klic hopping on a trampoline until Nico pulls the stop lever.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 2, 15. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Story code

```text
FOREVER: >     HOP
```

These are simplified pseudocode instructions.

## Try it together

Pretend to hop in a loop on a safe flat space. Agree on a stop signal before starting.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Does this forever loop stop on its own?

**Answer:** No. An outside stop or a programmed stopping condition is needed.

## For grown-ups

Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed.

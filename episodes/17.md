# Episode 17: Klic Makes a Backup

[Watch Episode 17](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/gmY3l9xVWgs)

**Concept:** backups  
**Learning goal:** Understand a backup as a separate copy used for recovery.

A drawing is copied to a second box before the first copy disappears, then restored.

## Learning path

After understanding files as saved work; use a pretend drawing.

## Try it together

Make two copies of a simple paper drawing; recover one from the second folder.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Which copy helps restore the drawing?

**Answer:** The saved backup.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

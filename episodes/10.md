# Episode 10: Push the Button!

[Watch Episode 10](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/TmNbK4eaaAE)

**Concept:** events  
**Learning goal:** Connect an event to its response.

Nico and Klic read ON CLICK → SPRAY, pause to predict, test waiting versus clicking, and recap the event and response.

## Learning path

After Episode 9 input and output.

## Try it together

Draw a button; tap it to cue a partner to wave.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Does waiting start the water? What starts it?

**Answer:** Waiting does not start the water. Clicking triggers the event and starts the spray.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

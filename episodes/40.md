# Episode 40: Which Door Hides the Star? Decision Tree

[Watch Episode 40](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/bJ5oaSI594U)

**Concept:** decision tree  
**Learning goal:** A decision tree follows answers, one choice at a time.

A decision tree guides Klic along two questions to the blue star. Follow the answers and compare the other paths.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 28. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Story code

```text
BLUE? > YES > STAR? > YES
```

These are simplified pseudocode instructions.

## Try it together

Make red-star, blue-circle and blue-star cards. Ask Is it blue? then Is it a star?

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Which two yes answers reach the blue star?

**Answer:** Yes to blue and yes to star.

## For grown-ups

Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed.

# Episode 9: Give Klic an Input!

[Watch Episode 9](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4czd_8Hmj4I)

**Concept:** Input and output  
**Learning goal:** Distinguish an incoming button press from the resulting movement.

A right-arrow press moves Klic right; a left-arrow press moves him left.

## Learning path

Combines instructions and observable results; leads to events.

## Try it together

Draw left and right buttons on paper. One person points to a button and another moves a toy accordingly.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Which part is the input?

**Answer:** The button press. Klic’s movement is the output.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

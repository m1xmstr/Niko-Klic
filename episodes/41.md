# Episode 41: Klic Lost His Drawing! Restore a Backup

Scheduled for October 07, 2026 at 09:20 PM Central. The video will become public at release.

**Concept:** backup  
**Learning goal:** Keep a backup. Restore it, then check your work.

A backup gives Klic a spare copy when his drawing goes missing. Restore the copy, then check the blue star matches.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 17, 39. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Story code

```text
KEEP A BACKUP COPY > RESTORE > CHECK
```

These are simplified pseudocode instructions.

## Try it together

Make two matching paper drawings. Keep one as a backup, hide the working drawing, then use the backup and compare.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What makes recovery possible in this example?

**Answer:** A backup copy was saved before the drawing was lost.

## For grown-ups

Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed.

# Episode 34: Everyone Can See It? Check Permissions

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** permissions  
**Learning goal:** Permissions control access. Ask a grown-up to help choose.

Klic has a pretend drawing to share with one chosen helper. Permissions control access. Ask a grown-up to help choose.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 12, 24. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Try it together

Use pretend name cards to choose who may see a drawing. Check the chosen name with a grown-up.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Should every drawing be shared with everyone?

**Answer:** No. Choose who should see it and check before sharing.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

# Episode 34: Who Can Open Klic's Art? Permissions

[Watch the episode](https://youtube.com/shorts/K5iRTslmD5s)

**Concept:** permissions  
**Learning goal:** Check permissions with a trusted grown-up before sharing.

Permissions decide who can view a drawing in Nico and Klic’s pretend gallery. One chosen door opens; the others stay locked.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 12, 24. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Story code

```text
ALLOW NICO: VIEW > KEEP OTHER ACCESS OFF
```

These are simplified pseudocode instructions.

## Try it together

Use pretend name cards to choose who may see a drawing. Check the chosen name with a grown-up.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Should every drawing be shared with everyone?

**Answer:** No. Choose who should see it and check before sharing.

## For grown-ups

Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed.

# Episode 20: One Step at a Time

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** algorithm  
**Learning goal:** Follow an ordered plan that reaches a goal.

Three highlighted instructions guide Klic right, across the bridge, then to collect one star.

## Learning path

Combines sequences, instructions and testing.

## Try it together

Draw a three-step toy route and follow each instruction in order.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What is an algorithm?

**Answer:** An ordered set of steps for completing a task.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

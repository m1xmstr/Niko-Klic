**Concept:** decimals  
**Learning goal:** Zero point five is one half. Decimals can show parts.

A measuring jar is half full, between zero and one. Zero point five is one half. Decimals can show parts.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 30. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Try it together

Draw a jar with zero, one-half and one marks. Shade half of the jar.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What decimal matches one-half?

**Answer:** 0.5.

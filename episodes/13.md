# Episode 13: Klic Sorts the Toys

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** sorting  
**Learning goal:** Sort items using one consistent rule.

Three balls visibly move into increasing size order.

## Learning path

After Episode 11 lists.

## Try it together

Arrange three safe household objects from small to large.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What rule did we use?

**Answer:** Smallest to largest.

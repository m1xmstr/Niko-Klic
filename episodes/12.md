# Episode 12: Ask Before You Share

[Watch Episode 12](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/4PdH-_KUqSs)

**Concept:** privacy  
**Learning goal:** Respect a no and ask permission before sharing someone’s photo.

A fictional photo pauses before sharing; permission is refused and the send button locks.

## Learning path

No coding prerequisites; introduces consent and privacy.

## Try it together

Use a drawing of a fictional character and practice asking permission.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What should Klic do after a no?

**Answer:** Keep the photo private and do not share.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

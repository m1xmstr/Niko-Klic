# Episode 22: The Missing Command

[Watch Episode 22](https://youtube.com/shorts/JyysqBYWOZs)

**Concept:** debugging  
**Learning goal:** Find a missing instruction, put it in the correct place, and test the code.

Klic walks past a star. Nico helps check the route, add TURN RIGHT before MOVE, and collect exactly one star.

## Learning path

Episodes 4 (sequences) and 10 (events). Revisit Episode 1 for the first debugging story.

## Try it together

Place a toy and a paper star on a pretend grid. Point the toy forward, add a right turn, then follow move and grab in order.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** Why does TURN RIGHT come before MOVE?

**Answer:** It changes which direction the next move will go. Moving first follows the old direction.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

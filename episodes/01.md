# Episode 1: Find the Bug!

[Watch Episode 1](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/N-2lVu0XPUw)

**Concept:** Debugging  
**Learning goal:** Spot an incorrect instruction and change RED to BLUE.

Nico and Klic review PRESS RED, find the incorrect color, and test PRESS BLUE.

## Learning path

No prerequisites; notice colors and instructions.

## Try it together

Choose a colored toy. Say a deliberately wrong color, correct it, and test again.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What changed in the code?

**Answer:** The color parameter changed from RED to BLUE.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

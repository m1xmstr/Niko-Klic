# Episode 38: Wait, It’s Unlocked! Device Security

Prepared; not yet publicly released.

**Concept:** device security  
**Learning goal:** Lock a device when you leave it. Ask a grown-up for help.

Klic finishes a drawing on a tablet and goes to play. Lock a device when you leave it. Ask a grown-up for help.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 16, 34. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Try it together

Ask a trusted grown-up to demonstrate locking their screen before stepping away. Do not share or practice real passwords.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What should Klic do before leaving his tablet?

**Answer:** Lock the screen, with grown-up help when needed.

## For grown-ups

Colored blocks are simplified pseudocode. Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed for these activities.

# Episode 38: Klic Left It Open! Lock the Screen

Scheduled for October 07, 2026 at 07:20 PM Central. The video will become public at release.

**Concept:** screen lock  
**Learning goal:** Stepping away? Lock your screen first.

A screen lock is the missing step when Klic races away from an open drawing app to play outside.

## Learning path

Earlier lessons: 16, 34. Revisit these ideas before trying this activity.

## Story code

```text
FINISHED USING IT? > LOCK THE SCREEN
```

These are simplified pseudocode instructions.

## Try it together

Ask a trusted grown-up to demonstrate locking their screen before stepping away. Do not share or practice real passwords.

## Check understanding

**Ask:** What should Klic do before leaving his tablet?

**Answer:** Lock the screen, with grown-up help when needed.

## For grown-ups

Pause, predict, and retell the steps together. Use pretend examples for safety lessons; never enter real passwords or private information. No account or paid tool is needed.
